## Step-specific requests

1. Put one whole uncooked pork-belly slab, ginger, scallion, and water in a deep pot; do not slice the pork.
2. Gently simmer the same whole slab and show chopstick and temperature checks without a rolling boil.
3. Prepare 3 cm green- and red-pepper strips plus 4 cm garlic-shoot or leek segments; no onion.
4. Cool and dry the cooked slab, then cut uniform 3 mm slices against the grain with skin and fat layers visible.
5. Render the thin slices until some fat releases and the flexible edges curl.
6. Push pork to one side and bloom red doubanjiang and small black fermented beans in the released oil on the other side.
7. Add the two pepper colours with soy, Shaoxing wine, and sugar; keep them bright and lightly crisp.
8. Add only long garlic-shoot or leek segments and cook until just softened; never substitute garlic cloves or scallions.
9. Serve curled thin pork, two-colour peppers, and garlic shoots in red oil without extra garnish.
