## Findings and changes

| Existing recipe | Finding | Change |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Beef chow fun (`beef-chow-fun`) | The ingredient list and expanded method disagreed on beef thickness (6 mm vs. 3 mm), while the thin cut was difficult to probe. Prep also listed a 30-minute beef marinade and 20-minute noodle rest sequentially, and the 10-minute cook estimate did not cover the full upper end of two searing batches plus noodle finishing. | Match the method to 6 mm flank strips, state that the sealed noodles rest during the refrigerated 30-minute marinade, and divide 15 ml searing oil between two uncrowded batches. Measure each batch to 63°C / 145°F with a thin-tip thermometer, rest at least 3 minutes, and continue in 15-second increments if needed. Return the rested meat and juices, divide the 280 g raw beef among four bowls (about 70 g raw-beef equivalent each), and set the displayed cook/total estimates to 14/54 minutes. Method and card now agree on shallow-container cooling within 2 hours at or below 4°C / 40°F, a two-day recipe limit, and reheating the portion served to 74°C / 165°F. |
| Hong Kong clay-pot rice (`clay-pot-rice`) | The final method did not divide the pot into the listed two servings, and its rice-specific leftover limits needed to appear in both the method and storage card. | Divide evenly between two bowls. For leftovers, use shallow containers, refrigerate within 1 hour, hold at or below 4°C / 40°F, use within 24 hours, and reheat rice only once until steaming hot throughout; briefly re-crisp the crust afterward. The 4°C ceiling is separately attributed to FoodSafety.gov general chilling guidance. |
| Taiwanese tea eggs (`taiwanese-tea-eggs`) | The method needed an explicit one-egg-per-serving yield, firm whites and yolks, and a bounded refrigerator steep and storage window. The existing 8–12-hour steep was longer than the displayed 495-minute prep estimate. | State eight eggs for eight servings and cook until whites and yolks are firm. Cool submerged eggs in two clean shallow containers with an ice bath, refrigerate at or below 4°C within 2 hours, steep 8–12 hours, and keep them covered in the broth. For warm portions, heat the largest egg to 74°C / 165°F, continuing in one-minute increments; serve one egg per person. Set prep/cook/total estimates to 735/55/790 minutes, so the prep estimate covers the 12-hour steep path plus estimated handling. The three-day storage limit is explicitly a conservative recipe choice, not an FDA value. |
| Moules marinières (`moules-marinieres`) | A 5–7-minute timer alone did not establish mussel doneness, and the shell-in amount was not allocated across four servings. | Keep live mussels at or below 4°C / 40°F; treat 5–7 minutes as a guide and use shell opening during cooking as the cue, discarding any that stay closed. Divide opened mussels as evenly as possible among four bowls; 1.5 kg shell-in is about 375 g per serving before discards. Method and card now agree on shallow-container cooling within 2 hours at or below 4°C, a one-day recipe limit, and reheating the serving to 74°C / 165°F. |

Portion equivalents, the 6 mm beef cut, searing intervals, steep duration, displayed time estimates, and the two-day, one-day, and three-day recipe limits are editorial choices for these recipe versions, not values taken from dish references or official guidance. The shell-in mussel division is a simple 1.5 kg ÷ 4 estimate before discards. The shorter leftover windows are conservative recipe limits. FoodSafety.gov's general leftover guidance supplies the 2-hour cooling, shallow-container, 4°C refrigerator, and 74°C reheating practices; it does not prescribe these dishes' exact storage-day limits.

## Official food-safety guidance

Official sources are separately labelled in recipe source data.

- [FoodSafety.gov — Safe Minimum Internal Temperatures](https://www.foodsafety.gov/food-safety-charts/safe-minimum-internal-temperatures): whole beef steaks at 63°C / 145°F followed by a three-minute rest; reheated leftovers at 74°C / 165°F.
- [FoodSafety.gov — 4 Steps to Food Safety](https://www.foodsafety.gov/keep-food-safe/4-steps-to-food-safety): refrigerate perishables within two hours, keep the refrigerator at or below 4°C / 40°F, and use shallow containers to cool leftovers promptly.
- [UK Food Standards Agency — Home food fact checker](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/home-food-fact-checker/home-food-fact-checker): cool cooked rice quickly, ideally within one hour; refrigerate and use within 24 hours; reheat once until steaming hot throughout.
- [FDA — What You Need to Know About Egg Safety](https://www.fda.gov/food/buy-store-serve-safe-food/what-you-need-know-about-egg-safety): refrigerate eggs at or below 4°C / 40°F, cook whites and yolks until firm, and reheat egg leftovers thoroughly. The recipe's three-day limit is a separately labelled conservative choice.
- [FDA — Selecting and Serving Fresh and Frozen Seafood Safely](https://www.fda.gov/food/buy-store-serve-safe-food/selecting-and-serving-fresh-and-frozen-seafood-safely): keep shellfish refrigerated; mussel shells should open during cooking, and unopened mussels should be discarded.
