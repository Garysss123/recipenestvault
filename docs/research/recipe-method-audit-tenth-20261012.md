## Source verification

Official safety reference:

- FoodSafety.gov, [Cook to a Safe Minimum Internal Temperature](https://www.foodsafety.gov/food-safety-charts/safe-minimum-internal-temperatures): fish reaches 63°C / 145°F, egg dishes reach 71°C / 160°F, and poultry reaches 74°C / 165°F. These values are applied only to the corresponding fish, egg-custard and chicken components. The chart does not establish any recipe's cook duration.

Dish and method references checked:

- [Turkish Red Lentil Soup — The Mediterranean Dish](https://www.themediterraneandish.com/turkish-lentil-soup/) and [Red Lentil Soup — Republic of Türkiye Ministry of Culture and Tourism](https://www.ktb.gov.tr/EN-99141/red-lentil-soup.html): checked for preparation, ingredients and cooking sequence. The Mediterranean Dish recipe supports the 5–7 minute vegetable stage, 4–5 minute boil and 15–20 minute simmer, but lists 5 tablespoons oil and cumin without paprika; the ministry version lists 2 tablespoons oil and paprika without cumin. The site's 45 ml oil, 5 g cumin and 3 g paprika, along with its salt split and finishing-butter quantities, are explicit formula choices rather than values attributed to either source.
- [Pierogi Ruskie — BBC Good Food](https://www.bbcgoodfood.com/recipes/pierogi-ruskie) and [Pierogi Ruskie — Polish Ministry of Agriculture and Rural Development](https://www.gov.pl/web/rolnictwo/pierogi-ruskie-z-chrzastawy): checked for the potato/onion preparation, filled-dumpling method and batch boiling. The BBC reference gives a 5–6 minute boil; this site's yield, allocation and 4–6 minute range are its own version choices.
- [Cambodian Fish Amok — Chef Nak](https://www.chefnak.com/cambodian-fish-amok) and [Fish Amok Recipe — Royal Embassy of Cambodia in London](https://www.cambodiaembassyuk.org/fish-amok-recipe/): checked for the banana-leaf-cup preparation and steaming method. The 15–18 minute guide is adapted for this site's four larger cups and is not represented as the source's timing.
- [Lao Chicken Larb — SBS Food](https://www.sbs.com.au/food/the-cook-up-with-adam-liaw/recipe/lao-chicken-larb/ph9p4bqen) and [Lao Chicken Larb — Australian Centre for International Agricultural Research](https://www.aciar.gov.au/sites/default/files/legacy/part_1701_p24-25_lao_chicken_larb.pdf): checked for the laap sequence, toasted-rice technique and serving with sticky rice and fresh vegetables. The SBS chicken cut, liquid and cook time differ from this site's ground-chicken method; site-specific quantities and timing are identified above.
