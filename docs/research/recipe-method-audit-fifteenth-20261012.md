## Findings and changes

| Recipe | Finding | Change |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Chicken and Sausage Gumbo | The chicken was only described as “season lightly,” no salt amount appeared in the ingredient list, and “cooked through” had no measured poultry endpoint. The listed 110-minute cook time equalled the low end of its timed stages, before the time needed to bring the stock to a simmer or any safety rechecks. | Add 6 g salt, allocated as 2 g for the 1.2 kg chicken and 4 g for the gumbo. Check the thickest thigh away from bone at 74°C / 165°F; if needed, keep simmering in 3-minute intervals and recheck. Increase cook/total estimates from 110/145 to 145/180 minutes. Divide the existing 800 g cooked rice into eight 100 g portions. |
| Pozole Rojo | “Large cubes” left the pork cut size variable, chile preparation followed a 75-minute pork simmer without saying the work could happen in parallel, pork completion relied on tenderness alone, and the listed garnishes were not apportioned. | Specify 4 cm pork cubes. Begin the chile steps during the first simmer. After the 35-minute hominy simmer, check the largest pork cube for 63°C / 145°F and fork tenderness; continue in 10-minute intervals if either is missing, then rest the pork in the broth off heat for 3 minutes. For eight servings, specify about 38 g cabbage, 22 g radish and 15 g onion per person and divide the soup among eight bowls. The existing 45/150/195-minute estimates remain. |
| Tortilla Española | The method intentionally left a moist, softly set egg centre but did not distinguish the safe approach for pasteurized versus ordinary shell eggs. The 15-minute prep estimate also omitted the method’s drain/cool and egg-mixture waits. Four servings were not translated into pieces per plate. | For a softly set centre, specify pasteurized eggs; with non-pasteurized eggs, check the centre for 71°C / 160°F and continue covered in 1-minute intervals if needed. Keep the soft-centre texture cue and do not add an oven temperature. Set prep/cook/total to 25/40/65 minutes; cut the four-serving tortilla into eight wedges, two per person. |
| Nürnberger Rostbratwürste with Sauerkraut | The 40-minute cook estimate did not cover the timed sequence: 7 minutes for onion/apple, 25 minutes braising, 5–8 minutes reducing, two 6–8-minute sausage batches, and a 3-minute rest (52–59 minutes total). The fresh sausages had no measured endpoint; the 6 g added salt could be too much for already-salted sauerkraut. | Increase cook/total to 65/80 minutes. Specify uncooked sausages and check the largest sausage centre at 71°C / 160°F, using 1-minute rechecks; direct cooks of packaged fully cooked links to the label. Reduce site-added salt to 3 g: 2 g with the onion/apple and the final 1 g only if needed after tasting. Allocate 700 g sauerkraut, 24 sausages and 40 g mustard across four plates: about 175 g kraut, six sausages and 10 g mustard per person. |

## Source verification

Official food-safety references:

- [FoodSafety.gov — Cook to a Safe Minimum Internal Temperature](https://www.foodsafety.gov/food-safety-charts/safe-minimum-internal-temperatures): 74°C / 165°F for poultry; 63°C / 145°F plus a 3-minute rest for whole cuts of pork; 71°C / 160°F for sausage; and 71°C / 160°F for egg dishes. The chart supplies safety endpoints, not recipe-specific cooking durations.
- [FDA — What You Need to Know About Egg Safety](https://www.fda.gov/food/buy-store-serve-safe-food/what-you-need-know-about-egg-safety): dishes with eggs should reach 160°F, while dishes served raw or undercooked should use pasteurized eggs or pasteurized egg products.

Recipe references checked:

- Chicken and Sausage Gumbo: [Food Network](https://www.foodnetwork.com/recipes/chicken-and-sausage-gumbo-recipe-2043183) and [Emeril Lagasse](https://www.emerils.com/127540/chicken-and-smoked-sausage-gumbo). They support the roux, sausage, chicken and rice dish context; their formulas and stage timings differ from this site's version.
- Pozole Rojo: [México in My Kitchen](https://www.mexicoinmykitchen.com/red-pozole-rojo/) and [Pati Jinich](https://patijinich.com/es/pozole-rojo/). They support the pork-and-hominy soup, chile sauce and fresh garnish sequence; neither is presented as the source of this site's quantities.
- Tortilla Española: [Spain.info](https://www.spain.info/en/recipe/potato-omelette/) and [Foods & Wines from Spain](https://www.foodswinesfromspain.com/en/fws-academy/recipes/2017/october/video-recipe--spanish-potato-omelet). They support the slow potato/onion preparation, egg mixture, two-sided pan set and serving context; their ratios and timings differ.
- Nürnberger Rostbratwürste: [Serious Eats — J. Kenji López-Alt](https://www.seriouseats.com/grilled-bratwurst-with-beer-mustard-and-sauerkraut-recipe) and the [Schutzverband Nürnberger Bratwürste](https://nuernberger-bratwuerste.de/de/erleben-geniessen/rezepte/gegrillte-nuernberger-bratwuerste-mit-fraenkischem-sauerkraut-und-holzofenbrot). They support the sausage-and-sauerkraut pairing and cooking sequence. Serious Eats uses a different beer-bath/grill method; neither reference supplies this site's quantities or 71°C official safety threshold.
