## Source verification

Official food-safety references:

- FoodSafety.gov, [Safe Minimum Internal Temperatures](https://www.foodsafety.gov/food-safety-charts/safe-minimum-internal-temperatures): chicken and other poultry reach 74°C / 165°F. This supports the Fesenjan chicken check only; it is not cited for the selected braise duration or for the other three recipes.
- FDA, [What You Need to Know About Egg Safety](https://www.fda.gov/food/buy-store-serve-safe-food/what-you-need-know-about-egg-safety): for recipes served with raw or undercooked eggs, use shell eggs treated to destroy Salmonella or pasteurized egg products. This is the basis for the Carbonara ingredient and method direction.

Dish and method references checked:

- [Fesenjan — The Washington Post](https://www.washingtonpost.com/recipes/fesenjan/) and [Chicken in Walnut-Pomegranate Sauce — Persian Mama](https://persianmama.com/chicken-in-walnut-pomegranate-sauce-khoresht-fesenjan/): checked for walnut/pomegranate braising, chicken, and rice service. Their braise variants support using a range rather than an exact source duration for the site's quantities. Persian Mama's linked rice method treats saffron as an optional small addition; it does not establish the site's 0.2 g amount.
- [Papushka's borscht by Olia Hercules — The Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/food/2020/sep/28/papushkas-borscht-by-olia-hercules) and [Ukrainian Winter Borscht — Country Life](https://www.countrylife.co.uk/food-drink/tom-parker-bowles-says-strap-in-its-borscht-season): checked as direct borshch references. The Guardian recipe uses roughly 2 kg pork ribs and a longer simmer; its timing was not transferred directly to this site's 500 g bite-size pork shoulder.
- Peace Corps Tanzania, [Kumbe, I Can Cook](https://www.maktaba.org/download/file/772/Kumbe_i_can_cook_Peace_corps_tanzania.pdf), and [Tanzanian Pilau — Koki Afrique](https://kokiafrique.com/en/dishes/pilau/): checked for pilau ingredients and sequence. Koki's smaller recipe provides the stated onion and rice time ranges; the site's 700 g beef time remains an explicit adaptation.
- [Spaghetti alla Carbonara — GialloZafferano](https://ricette.giallozafferano.it/Spaghetti-alla-Carbonara.html) and [Spaghetti alla carbonara — La Cucina Italiana](https://www.lacucinaitaliana.it/ricetta/primi/spaghetti-alla-carbonara-2/): checked for the Roman carbonara ingredient and off-heat emulsion method. FDA supplies the egg-safety direction, not the culinary technique.
