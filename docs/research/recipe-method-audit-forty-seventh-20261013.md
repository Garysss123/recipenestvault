## Findings and changes

| Existing recipe | Finding | Change |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Idli sambar (`idli-sambar`) | Soaks and grinding water were not fully sequenced or allocated; the method did not define mold yield, batch timing, or per-person sides. Storage guidance did not distinguish cooked rice cakes, stew, and unsteamed fermented batter. | Run the 5–6-hour rice soak alongside the separate four-hour urad/fenugreek soak. Grind each with its own measured water (220–280 ml and 430–500 ml) for 8–10 minutes; together these account for the listed 650–780 ml batter water. Assign the 850 ml sambar water as 550 ml for dal and 300 ml for vegetables, then combine without adding unmeasured water. Steam 18 idli in batches, 10–12 minutes each, and portion three per person plus 40 g chutney. Storage now applies the FSA cooked-rice advice to cooked idli; sambar and fermented batter have separate conservative recipe limits. Added a dedicated idli-specific common-mistakes card. |
| Shoyu ramen (`shoyu-ramen`) | The pork time was presented as if it guaranteed both doneness and tenderness, with an incorrect 71°C endpoint for whole-cut pork. Broth, toppings, and leftover handling needed measured per-bowl amounts. | Use a thermometer at the thickest centre: the official whole-cut pork minimum is 63°C / 145°F followed by a three-minute rest. The initial 45-minute braise and optional 10-minute tenderness increments are labelled recipe estimates. Divide 600 g noodles among four bowls (150 g each), allocate about 300 ml stock plus 37.5 ml tare, two of eight chashu slices (about 125 g raw-pork equivalent), 30 g menma, 20 g narutomaki, and 15 g scallions per bowl. Cool pork, broth, and tare separately in shallow containers within two hours; the two-day limit is explicitly a conservative recipe choice. |
| Königsberger Klopse (`koenigsberger-klopse`) | The largest meatball lacked an explicit measured endpoint and fallback check. The sauce step did not say that half the listed poaching stock is not used in sauce; salt could be over-added despite anchovy and capers. | Check the largest meatball from the side at 71°C / 160°F, the official ground-meat minimum. Treat 15–18 minutes as a guide and continue in two-minute increments if needed. Measure 600 ml stock for sauce and save or discard the remaining 600 ml separately. Add up to the remaining 6 g salt only after tasting. Divide 12 meatballs as three per plate. Storage covers the meatballs, sauce, and optional saved stock separately; three days is a conservative recipe limit. |
| Traditional trifle (`traditional-trifle`) | The jelly chill and custard preparation were described as consecutive even though they can overlap; warm custard layering and the egg-safety meaning of the 85°C texture target needed clarification. Serving garnish allocation was not stated. | Prepare custard while the jelly sets, chill it separately, and layer only when fully cold. Keep the 85°C target as this recipe's starch-thickening texture target and state the official egg-dish minimum of 71°C / 160°F separately. Move custard to a shallow container and refrigerate within two hours of cooking. Divide into ten portions; optional 40 g almonds are about 4 g per serving. The two-day chilled-storage limit is labelled a conservative recipe choice. |

The 990-minute idli prep estimate covers the overlapping soak, grinding, and a 10-hour midpoint fermentation plan; fermentation can take 8–12 hours, so elapsed time varies. Its 70-minute cooking figure is a recipe planning estimate for dal, vegetables, and steaming. Ramen's 45-minute braise and ten-minute tenderness increments are estimates, separate from the official pork temperature and rest. The 85°C custard value is for texture, not an official safety value. The 2-day idli-sambar, 3-day Klopse, and 2-day trifle limits are conservative recipe choices. These estimates and limits are not official values.

## Official food-safety guidance

Official sources are separately labelled in each recipe's source data. The guidance below was applied only to the relevant ingredients or storage steps.

- [FoodSafety.gov — Safe Minimum Internal Temperatures](https://www.foodsafety.gov/food-safety-charts/safe-minimum-internal-temperatures): whole cuts of pork at 63°C / 145°F followed by a three-minute rest; ground meat at 71°C / 160°F; egg dishes at 71°C / 160°F; leftovers reheated to 74°C / 165°F.
- [FoodSafety.gov — 4 Steps to Food Safety](https://www.foodsafety.gov/keep-food-safe/4-steps-to-food-safety): refrigerate perishables within two hours, use shallow containers, and keep the refrigerator at or below 4°C / 40°F.
- [UK Food Standards Agency — Home food fact checker](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/home-food-fact-checker/home-food-fact-checker): cool cooked rice quickly, ideally refrigerate within one hour, use within 24 hours, and reheat only once until steaming hot throughout. Applied to cooked idli; this rule is not extended to uncooked fermented batter.
- [FoodSafety.gov — Cold Food Storage Charts](https://www.foodsafety.gov/food-safety-charts/cold-food-storage-charts): general chilled-storage reference. The shorter dish-specific limits in this batch are identified as recipe choices.
