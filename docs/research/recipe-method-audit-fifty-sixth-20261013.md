## Official food-safety guidance

Official safety references are labelled separately from culinary recipe references in the recipe data:

- [FoodSafety.gov — Safe Minimum Internal Temperatures](https://www.foodsafety.gov/food-safety-charts/safe-minimum-internal-temperatures): whole pork steaks/chops/roasts at 63°C / 145°F followed by a 3-minute rest; ground meat, including pork, at 71°C / 160°F; fish at 63°C / 145°F or opaque and readily separable; leftovers reheated to 74°C / 165°F. These distinct cases are used only for the relevant food in these recipes.
- [FDA — What You Need to Know About Egg Safety](https://www.fda.gov/food/buy-store-serve-safe-food/what-you-need-know-about-egg-safety): cook whites and yolks until firm; use pasteurized eggs for undercooked preparations; refrigerate cooked eggs promptly.
- [FoodSafety.gov — 4 Steps to Food Safety](https://www.foodsafety.gov/keep-food-safe/4-steps-to-food-safety): refrigerate perishables within two hours, maintain refrigerator temperature at or below 4°C / 40°F, and use shallow containers for faster cooling.
- [FoodSafety.gov — Cold Food Storage Charts](https://www.foodsafety.gov/food-safety-charts/cold-food-storage-charts): 3–4 days for soups/stews, cooked leftovers, and prepared egg/chicken/ham/tuna salads, as applicable.
- [UK Food Standards Agency — Home Food Fact Checker](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/home-food-fact-checker/home-food-fact-checker): cool cooked rice as quickly as possible, ideally within one hour; refrigerate and use within 24 hours; reheat only once until steaming hot. Checked 2026-10-13; source page was updated 2026-08-21.
