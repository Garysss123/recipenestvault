## Recipe source cross-check

### Chicken karaage

- [Kikkoman — Crispy Karaage Recipe (Japanese Fried Chicken)](https://www.kikkoman.com/en/cookbook/washoku/karaage.html): 600 g thigh meat, soy/sake/ginger/garlic seasoning, flour coating, 160°C first fry, small batches, and crisp-frying guidance.
- [Just One Cookbook — Karaage (Japanese Fried Chicken)](https://www.justonecookbook.com/karaage/): 5 cm thigh pieces, soy/sake/ginger/garlic/sesame marinade, potato starch plus flour, 160°C first fry and 180°C second fry, with overcrowding and draining guidance.

The published method is an original standardized rewrite of both sources: 4 cm pieces, weighed seasonings, 30-minute refrigeration, separate flour and potato-starch dishes, 3–5 pieces per batch, 160°C for about 90 seconds, 180°C for 60–90 seconds, clean rack drainage, and a 74°C thickest-centre safety check. Raw marinade and raw/cooked equipment separation are explicit additions for reproducibility and cross-contamination control. The selected photo visibly shows golden karaage on large torn green cabbage leaves; the recipe lists exactly 60 g of that serving side and does not claim visible lemon, mayonnaise, sesame, sauce, or other garnish.

### Tonkatsu

- [Kikkoman — Tonkatsu (Japanese Pork Cutlet)](https://www.kikkoman.com/en/cookbook/washoku/tonkatsu.html): 2 cm pork loin, flour/egg/panko breading, 170°C frying, cabbage, mustard, and homemade tonkatsu sauce.
- [Just One Cookbook — Crispy Tonkatsu (Japanese Pork Cutlet)](https://www.justonecookbook.com/tonkatsu/): connective-tissue scoring and tenderizing, egg wash with water, 170°C first fry, 180°C finishing guidance, wire-rack drainage, cabbage, and sauce.

The published method is an original standardized rewrite of both sources: two 2 cm chops/300 g, weighed breading and sauce components, 10-minute breading rest, one-chop batches at 170°C for 3 minutes plus 2 minutes after flipping, clean-rack drainage, and a 68°C thickest-centre check followed by a 3-minute rest. The selected photo visibly shows sliced panko tonkatsu with shredded cabbage and yellow mustard; the recipe lists those elements and does not claim the rice, soup, pickles, or other set-meal items that are outside the reviewed crop.
